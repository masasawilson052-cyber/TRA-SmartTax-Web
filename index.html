Tax compliance made simpler for every small business.

KodiRahisi AI helps Tanzanian entrepreneurs organize daily records, understand common tax steps and prepare for official TRA services. The prototype demonstrates Kiswahili/English guidance, receipt review and low-data-friendly workflows; production AI, voice and live TRA integrations would require formal approval.

Independent innovation prototype proposed for consideration by the Tanzania Revenue Authority.

This demonstration is not an official TRA production system.

The Problem We Are Solving

Many micro and small businesses may face difficulties such as inconsistent business records, limited understanding of tax procedures, missed filing deadlines, difficulty separating business and personal transactions, limited access to professional tax advice, language and digital-literacy barriers, and time-consuming manual processes.

Current Experience

  • Paper receipts become lost
  • Complicated tax terminology
  • Deadlines are forgotten
  • Records are assembled late
  • Routine questions require assistance

With KodiRahisi AI

  • Receipts are photographed and organized
  • Plain Kiswahili or English explanations
  • Personalized reminders
  • Daily records create monthly summaries
  • Guided assistance answers routine questions and points to official services

Pilot, Safety & Evidence

How the innovation would be tested, governed and scaled only with evidence.

What the pilot should prove

The proposal does not assume that technology alone increases revenue. The pilot should test whether better records, clearer guidance and earlier action produce measurable improvements.

Record completenessAre monthly records more complete?
On-time completionDo users meet routine obligations earlier?
Task timeDoes the service reduce time spent on selected processes?
Correction rateHow often do users correct proposed extraction?
Officer escalationWhich routine questions still need human support?
Adoption & trustActive use, satisfaction, dropout and complaint signals.

Six-Month Controlled Pilot

Recommended scope: 1,000 micro and small businesses • two regions • four business sectors • six months.
01Baseline

Measure current records, time, support needs and compliance experience.

02Onboarding

Consent, training, supported first use and clear privacy notice.

03Live pilot

Daily record capture, guided assistance and reminders using approved content.

04Midpoint review

Accuracy, usability, security, adoption and corrective actions.

05End-line

Compare outcomes with baseline and a suitable comparison approach.

06Decision gate

Independent review: scale, revise or stop.

Scale only with evidence. Production integration should proceed only after legal authority, security, AI quality, usability and operating readiness meet agreed thresholds.

Privacy, Cybersecurity & Responsible AI

Trust must be designed into every transaction. This public prototype intentionally uses no real taxpayer login and sends no uploaded receipt to a backend.

Identity & access

Production: TIN-based verification where approved, MFA/OTP, role-based access and session controls.

Privacy

Clear purpose, data minimisation, retention limits, data-subject rights and privacy impact assessment.

Security

Encryption, secure development, logging, monitoring, incident response, backup and recovery testing.

AI governance

Approved sources, versioned rules/models, evaluation, confidence thresholds, reason codes and human escalation.

Taxpayer control

Review and correction before a record is accepted or transmitted. Clear complaint and appeal routes.

Auditability

Traceable actions, change control and independent assurance before handling live taxpayer data.

Non-negotiable boundary: AI must not autonomously impose a liability, issue a penalty or transmit taxpayer information without an approved legal and operational basis.
