TRA Proposal Documentation

Business case, implementation roadmap, and security compliance.

Strategic Impact on Tax Administration

  • Increased Tax Compliance: By simplifying tax calculation and record-keeping through an intuitive AI interface, small business owners are empowered to comply voluntarily, reducing accidental evasion caused by complexity.
  • Reduction of the Informal Sector: The barrier to entry for formalization is lowered. Free TIN registration guidance and Virtual EFD capabilities encourage informal traders to register and operate formally.
  • Reduced Administrative Costs: The AI Assistant serves as a first-line support agent, dramatically decreasing the volume of routine queries to TRA call centers and branch offices.
  • Data-Driven Insights: Automated receipt scanning and digital records provide TRA with structured, verifiable data, enhancing audit capabilities and economic forecasting.

Implementation Roadmap

Month 1: Prototype Refinement & API Design

Finalize the UI/UX based on TRA stakeholder feedback. Design secure API endpoints for integration with existing TRA databases.

Month 2-3: GePG & e-Filing Integration

Securely connect the platform to the Government e-Payment Gateway (GePG) for instant tax payments and the TRA e-Filing system for automated returns.

Month 4-6: Controlled Rollout

Launch a pilot program targeting 5,000 SME users in Dar es Salaam. Monitor server loads, gather user feedback, and measure compliance uplift.

Security & Compliance Architecture

We recognize that taxpayer data is highly sensitive. KodiRahisi AI is designed from the ground up to exceed government security requirements.

End-to-End Encryption

All data in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256) is fully encrypted to prevent unauthorized access.

Local Data Residency

All databases and processing servers will be physically hosted within the United Republic of Tanzania.

eGA Compliance

Strict adherence to the e-Government Authority (eGA) standards and the Tanzania Data Protection Act.
